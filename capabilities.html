Engineering & technical capability

The technical
department.

Our technical teams provide engineering coordination, design review, technical documentation, surveying, structural and civil engineering support, and geotechnical input.

They handle shop drawing coordination, material submittals, method statements, technical clarifications and site engineering assistance. Working closely with project management, the technical department translates engineering requirements into practical, controlled construction activities.

Geotechnical engineering

What is under
the building.

MERAAS provides integrated geotechnical engineering and surveying services through experienced professionals and modern technology.

Capabilities include borehole drilling, SPT/CPT, soil and rock testing, geotechnical investigations, topographic and as-built surveys, and construction setting-out. Supported by advanced drilling rigs, GNSS/RTK GPS, total stations, drones/UAVs and modern testing equipment, we deliver accurate and reliable solutions for buildings, roads, infrastructure and major development projects.

TBZ Headquarters

Contemporary corporate architecture — an inspiring headquarters for modern business.

Location Benghazi, Libya
Category Commercial & Corporate
Primary Scope Corporate Office Building, Structural Glazing & Executive Workspace

Project Overview

High-performance structural glazing, open-plan collaborative floors, and executive boardroom suites.

A striking commercial headquarters engineered to project modern corporate leadership, featuring floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass, flexible open-plan workspaces, and robust infrastructure.

The architectural design emphasizes natural daylight, thermal efficiency, and acoustic separation between executive zones and operational trading floors. MERAAS handled all structural engineering, façade installation, interior construction, and high-security access controls.

Constructed to top international corporate standards, the building integrates central IT server rooms with redundant power, conference telepresence suites, and sustainable LED lighting.

Key Engineering Highlights
  • High-Performance Acoustic Structural Glazing
  • Flexible Open-Plan Office Floorplates
  • Tier-2 Redundant Power & Server Facilities
  • Executive Penthouse Boardroom & Terraces

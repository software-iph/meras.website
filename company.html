About us

MERAAS Engineering
& Construction.

A Libya-based engineering and construction company providing integrated engineering, construction, infrastructure, geotechnical, project management and heavy equipment services.

With approximately 550 employees, MERAAS combines engineering expertise, practical experience, modern equipment, quality management and strong project coordination to deliver reliable, efficient and high-quality solutions across Libya.

At MERAAS we believe in building with purpose. Our multidisciplinary team combines international experience, technical expertise and a strong commitment to quality — and we focus on delivering projects that not only meet today’s needs but also shape a better future through precision, resilience and innovation.

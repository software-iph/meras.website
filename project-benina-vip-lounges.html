Benina Airport — VIP Lounges

Project Overview

Sculptural wood cladding, integrated ambient lighting, and bespoke terrazzo flooring for dignitary reception.

The complete architectural design and turnkey fit-out of the dignitary and executive VIP reception lounges at Benina International Airport in Benghazi, blending Libyan hospitality with timeless contemporary luxury.

The interior architecture is characterized by sweeping curved American walnut walls, cove LED lighting, custom acoustic plaster ceilings, high-traffic terrazzo flooring, and bespoke modular seating designed for utmost privacy and acoustic tranquility.

MERAAS managed all bespoke millwork fabrication, specialized security glass, independent HVAC zoning for air quality and silence, private meeting suites, and discreet diplomatic reception counters.

Key Engineering Highlights
  • Sculptural Curved Timber Wall Architecture
  • Seamless Terrazzo Poured Flooring with Brass Inlays
  • Ultra-Quiet Acoustic Ceiling & HVAC Zoning
  • Private Diplomatic Suites & Executive Meeting Rooms

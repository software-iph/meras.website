Our Services

One organisation,
every discipline.

MERAAS combines engineering expertise, construction experience, geotechnical capability, heavy equipment resources, project management, quality assurance and HSE management within one integrated organisation.

That combination lets us keep strong control over resources, technical requirements, quality, safety and delivery, instead of coordinating it across four contractors.

01

Civil Works

Structural and civil construction across residential, commercial, institutional and mixed-use developments, from substructure to handover.

02

Infrastructure Development

Utilities, drainage, site services and the coordinated infrastructure that makes large developments operable.

03

Road Construction

Earthworks, sub-base, paving and surfacing for urban and inter-urban routes, delivered with our own plant.

04

Earthworks & Site Development

Excavation, grading, compaction and bulk earthmoving, supported by a dedicated machinery fleet and experienced operators.

05

Geotechnical Engineering

Borehole drilling, SPT/CPT, soil and rock testing, geotechnical investigation, topographic and as-built survey, and construction setting-out.

06

Project Management

Coordination across every engineering discipline, procurement and site team to keep programme, cost and quality under one command.
